Our Story

A bakery built on flour, fire, and time.

Crumb Story began in a small home kitchen with one starter, two hands, and the conviction that great bread deserves patience. A decade later we're still fermenting overnight, shaping by hand, and baking on stone — because some things shouldn't be rushed.

Every loaf, croissant and cake leaves the oven carrying the same idea: real ingredients, real craft, real time.
